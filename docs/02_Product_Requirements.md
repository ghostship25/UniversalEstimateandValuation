# 02. Product Requirements Document (PRD)

## Product Overview
Universal Estimating & Valuation is an enterprise-grade Rate Analysis, Resource Master, and Project BOQ Cost Estimation software tailored for 's construction engineering environment.

## Target Stakeholders & User Personas
1. **Government Estimating Engineer**: Needs to compute official Engineer Estimates based on approved departmental norms (BLD / RDS / IRR).
2. **Zone Rate Committee Officer**: Responsible for uploading and updating annual zone wage and material rate schedules across 's 77 zones.
3. **Contractor Cost Estimator**: Prepares competitive tender bids, customizes resource prices, and runs sensitivity / profit analysis.
4. **Auditor / Vigilance Officer**: Inspects rate breakdowns for unauthorized formula tampering, abnormal resource prices, or duplicate line items.

## Core Modules
- **Master Data Governance**: Material, Labor, Equipment, Transport, Zone, Local Authority, and Unit Masters.
- **Standard Norms Library**: Pre-loaded with Standard Building Norms and RDS Road & Bridge Norms with exact resource coefficients.
- **Dynamic Rate Analysis Engine**: Interactive calculation worksheet with Lead/Lift, Royalty, Machine Hour rates, overheads, and Tax.
- **Project BOQ & Abstract Engine**: Full project hierarchy with WBS, Quantity take-offs, and Abstract of Cost summaries.
- **Data Normalization & Audit**: AI & rule-based duplicate detection, alias matching, unit standardization, and spelling correction.
- **Import/Export Suite**: Support for bulk Excel, CSV, JSON, and printable official PDF reports.
