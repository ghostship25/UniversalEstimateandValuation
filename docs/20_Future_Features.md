# 20. Future Features & Long-term Vision

## Enterprise Scale & 20-Year Horizon
1. **AI OCR PDF & Drawing Scanner**: Extract BOQ line items and quantities directly from structural CAD/PDF drawings.
2. **Blockchain / Cryptographic Audit Trail**: Immutable ledger of government approved norm revisions to prevent corruption or silent rate modifications.
3. **Automated Price Escalation Engine**: Real-time  national consumer price index (CPI) integration for computing Price Adjustment Clause values during multi-year contracts.
4. **GIS Site Distance Calculator**: Auto-calculate material haulage distance (Lead km) based on project GPS coordinates and nearest quarry/factory locations.
